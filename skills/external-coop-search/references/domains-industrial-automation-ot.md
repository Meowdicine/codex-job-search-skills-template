- Student/co-op roles involving software, data, dashboards, monitoring, control-system-adjacent analysis, plant data, simulation, reporting, or automation.

## Manual Review

- Licensed trades, professional engineering license requirements, heavy electrical/mechanical maintenance, field service with vehicle requirements, rotating shifts, or safety credentials the candidate lacks.
