# Data Center And Infrastructure Watchlist Template

Customize for the candidate's geography and eligibility.

## Search Families

- Hyperscaler/cloud infrastructure roles.
- Data center operations student, intern, trainee, or junior roles.
- Network operations, NOC, observability, monitoring, and infrastructure tooling.
- Critical environment, facility operations, power/cooling, and building systems roles where student eligibility is plausible.
- AI infrastructure, GPU cloud, infrastructure automation, and platform roles.

## Search Terms

- `data center intern Canada`
- `data center co-op Canada`
- `network operations intern Canada`
- `cloud infrastructure intern Canada`
- `critical environment intern`
- `facility operations trainee data center`
- `AI infrastructure intern`
- `observability intern infrastructure`

## Hard Gates

Treat these as manual review or skip triggers:

- security clearance
- long residency requirement
- citizenship/PR requirement
- driver's license/vehicle
- rotating shifts
- heavy lifting
- electrical/mechanical trade credential
- licensed trade
- 2+ years data center operations
- 4+ years facility operations
- remote/rural relocation
- bilingual/French requirement, unless supported

## Good Signals

- student/intern/co-op label
- paid training
- entry-level/junior label
- cloud/platform/software/data responsibilities
- monitoring, automation, scripting, dashboards, internal tools
- employer can document work term details for school approval
